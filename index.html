About Rent Ledger

Rent Ledger helps property owners organize tenant details, rent due dates, payments, receipts, and agreement renewals in one place.

Reminder and receipt links open WhatsApp for you to review and send. Your ledger is saved in this browser and can sync to your account when you sign in.

Install Rent Ledger

If your browser does not show the automatic install prompt:

  • Android / Chrome: open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • iPhone / Safari: tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.
Installation requires the website to be served securely over HTTPS.
